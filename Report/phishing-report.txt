SAMPLE PHISHING EMAIL

Subject:
Urgent: Your Account Will Be Locked

Email Body:

Dear User,

We noticed suspicious activity on your account.
To avoid account suspension, please verify your details immediately.

Verify Now:
http://secure-account-verify.com

Failure to verify within 24 hours will result in permanent account lock.

Regards,
Security Team

---

PHISHING INDICATOR ANALYSIS

1. Suspicious Sender & Domain
   Risk Level: High

Observation:
The email references a suspicious verification domain that does not appear to belong to a legitimate organization.

Why It Is Dangerous:
Attackers commonly use fake domains that imitate trusted services to steal credentials and sensitive information.

---

2. Urgency & Fear-Based Language
   Risk Level: Medium

Observation:
The message creates urgency by threatening account suspension within 24 hours.

Why It Is Dangerous:
Fear and urgency pressure users into acting quickly without verifying legitimacy.

---

3. Generic Greeting
   Risk Level: Medium

Observation:
The email uses a generic greeting ("Dear User") instead of the recipient's actual name.

Why It Is Dangerous:
Mass phishing campaigns commonly use generic greetings because attackers do not know recipient identities.

---

4. Suspicious Link
   Risk Level: High

Observation:
The included verification link uses a suspicious and misleading domain structure.

Why It Is Dangerous:
Users clicking such links may be redirected to fake login pages designed to steal credentials.

---

5. Social Engineering Techniques
   Risk Level: Medium

Observation:
The attacker attempts to manipulate user emotions using fear, urgency, and account security concerns.

Why It Is Dangerous:
Social engineering increases the likelihood of users responding without caution.

---

RISK CLASSIFICATION

Final Classification:
PHISHING EMAIL

Reason:
The analyzed email demonstrates multiple phishing characteristics, including suspicious links, urgency tactics, fake domain indicators, and social engineering methods.

Overall Risk:
High

PREVENTION & AWARENESS GUIDELINES

Employees and users should follow the following security practices to reduce phishing risks:

1. Verify Sender Addresses
   Always check sender email domains carefully before clicking links or responding.

2. Avoid Clicking Suspicious Links
   Hover over links before opening them and verify legitimacy.

3. Be Cautious of Urgent Messages
   Attackers often use fear and urgency to manipulate users into acting quickly.

4. Never Share Passwords or OTPs
   Legitimate organizations do not request passwords or OTPs through email.

5. Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
   Enable MFA to reduce the impact of stolen credentials.

6. Report Suspicious Emails
   Users should immediately report suspicious emails to IT or security teams.

---

DO'S AND DON'TS

DO:

* Verify email authenticity
* Confirm suspicious requests through official channels
* Keep systems and browsers updated
* Use strong passwords

DON'T:

* Click unknown links
* Download suspicious attachments
* Share credentials through email
* Trust urgent threats without verification
